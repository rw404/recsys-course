\section{Operate for failure and change}

\begin{abstract}
A good design explains refresh, observability, degradation and rollback before launch.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Concept}
Document model and index refresh cadence, feature freshness limits, cache behavior and fallbacks. Monitor latency, errors, score drift, candidate-source mix, concentration and outcome shifts. Canary releases and rollback paths reduce blast radius. Ownership matters as much as architecture: alerts need thresholds, runbooks and a team able to act. Production quality is the ability to remain understandable while data and behavior change.

\begin{equation*}
observe \rightarrow detect \rightarrow degrade safely \rightarrow diagnose lineage \rightarrow rollback or repair
\end{equation*}

\subsection{Key terms}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Canary.} A limited rollout used to detect failures before broad release.
\item \textbf{Drift.} A change in data, scores or outcomes relative to the expected distribution.
\item \textbf{Runbook.} An operational procedure for diagnosing and responding to an alert.
\end{itemize}
