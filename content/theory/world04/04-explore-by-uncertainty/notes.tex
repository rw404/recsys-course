\section{Explore by uncertainty}

\begin{abstract}
UCB and Thompson Sampling focus exploration where the answer is still uncertain.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Concept}
Upper Confidence Bound adds an optimism bonus that is largest for rarely selected arms. As evidence accumulates, the bonus shrinks and the policy concentrates on strong actions. Thompson Sampling maintains a posterior for each action, samples a plausible reward from each and chooses the winner. Both direct exploration using uncertainty instead of spending a fixed random fraction everywhere.

\begin{equation*}
UCBₜ(a) = Q̂ₜ(a) + c √(ln t / Nₜ(a))
\end{equation*}

\subsection{Key terms}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Confidence bound.} A range expressing uncertainty around an estimated reward.
\item \textbf{Posterior.} A probability distribution over an unknown quantity after observing data.
\item \textbf{Thompson Sampling.} Choosing actions by sampling from their reward posteriors.
\end{itemize}
