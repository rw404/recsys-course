\section{Optimize for long-term value}

\begin{abstract}
A policy can win the next click while quietly losing trust, retention or creator supply.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Concept}
Immediate engagement is easy to observe and therefore easy to over-optimize. Clickbait, excessive notifications or repetitive autoplay can lift a short metric while increasing fatigue and churn. Long-term objectives combine retention, satisfaction, successful task completion and healthy content supply. Delayed outcomes are noisy, so teams use leading indicators, holdouts and cohort analysis rather than pretending one scalar reward captures everything.

\begin{equation*}
long-term value \approx immediate utility + γ \cdot future user and ecosystem value
\end{equation*}

\subsection{Key terms}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Churn.} A user becoming inactive or leaving the product.
\item \textbf{Guardrail metric.} A metric that must not degrade while optimizing a primary objective.
\item \textbf{Cohort.} A group tracked over time from a shared start or treatment.
\end{itemize}
